# Code of conduct

VersaTul contributors, maintainers, and participants are expected to keep
project interactions respectful and useful. This applies to public issues,
pull requests, documentation contributions, and project-related private
communications.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss technical disagreements with care and respect.
- Keep feedback specific to the work and its effects.
- Give others a fair chance to clarify intent and correct mistakes.
- Protect private information and credentials.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, or repeated unwanted
  contact.
- Publishing another person's private information or credentials.
- Deliberately disrupting project discussions or repeatedly posting unrelated
  material.

## Reporting and enforcement

The DocsVersaTul repository owner and maintainers handle conduct concerns. To
raise one privately, open a package from the [VersaTul NuGet
profile](https://www.nuget.org/profiles/bwiliams256) and use **Contact owners**
to request a private channel; share details after the maintainer confirms that
channel. Do not use a public issue for sensitive conduct reports. For a
security vulnerability, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may ask for a correction, remove or close harmful content, warn a
participant, or restrict project access when needed to protect the community.
There is no guaranteed response timeline.
